Our Hiring Process

Structured framework from requirement to onboarding

01

Step 1 - Requirement Analysis

Understand institutional goals, subject needs, role scope, and hiring timelines.

02

Step 2 - Teacher Sourcing

Source candidates through our educator database, network outreach, and referrals.

03

Step 3 - Screening & Shortlisting

Run qualification checks, HR screening, demo assessment, and verification workflows.

04

Step 4 - Interview & Selection

Institution teams conduct final interviews with high-fit shortlisted candidates.

05

Step 5 - Onboarding & Support

Coordinate joining, documentation, and replacement support when required.

This process helps institutions reduce hiring time, improve candidate quality, and ensure academic alignment.
